BUG-903: Introspect endpoint accepts expired tokens during clock skew
Meridian Systems  |  Issue Tracker Export
Last updated: 2025-09-22

Status: fixed. Severity: high. Customer impact: none observed.

BUG-903 affects Ember. The /introspect endpoint validated token expiry against the local node
clock with a five minute tolerance intended to absorb clock skew between regions. The
tolerance was applied in both directions, so a token that had genuinely expired up to five
minutes earlier still validated.

BUG-903 was reported by Nadia Petrova during the review that followed INC-2103, while she was
reading the auth path for logging violations rather than for logic errors.

BUG-903 was fixed by Marcus Chen. This was a cross-team fix, since Ember is owned by the
Security Team and Marcus Chen is a member of the Platform Team; it proceeded under the
cross-team exception with Tomas Oliveira as reviewer. The tolerance is now applied only in
the direction that absorbs skew for not-yet-valid tokens, and expiry is strict.

The five minute revocation confusion this created is distinct from the documented thirty
second eventual consistency of /revoke.
