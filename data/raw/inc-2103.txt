INC-2103: Ember Token Material in Logs
Meridian Systems  |  Incident Review
Last updated: 2025-08-16

Severity 2. No confirmed external exploitation, but treated as a credential exposure.

INC-2103 was caused by Ember. A debug logging statement added during a performance
investigation wrote the full request body of the /introspect endpoint at INFO level. Request
bodies to that endpoint contain complete bearer tokens. The statement shipped on a Tuesday
and ran in production for nine days before anyone noticed.

Because logs are shipped to Beacon and Beacon retains raw events for thirty days, the
exposure surface was every engineer with dashboard access, plus the vendor that operates the
visualization layer.

INC-2103 was reported by Nadia Petrova during a routine log sampling review. INC-2103 was
resolved by Tomas Oliveira, who purged the affected partitions, forced revocation of every
token issued in the window, and added a pre-commit check that fails on any log statement
referencing a request body in the auth path.

INC-2103 triggered the Credential Rotation Standard. It is also the origin of the rule that
Ember never logs raw token material under any log level.
