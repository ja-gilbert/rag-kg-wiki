Runbook: Cinder
Meridian Systems  |  Operations Runbook
Last updated: 2025-10-11

Cinder runs on spot capacity. Evictions are normal and expected; the fleet is sized assuming
a steady background rate of them.

If tier one jobs are missing their one hour budget, check queue depth before checking worker
health. There is no global backpressure, so a shortage of workers does not slow the rate at
which jobs are submitted, and the queue can grow past the point where catching up is possible
within the budget. The manual intervention is to pause tier three submissions.

Never submit a backfill whose output range overlaps the live ingestion window. Output
partitions are idempotent overwrites, not merges, so an overlapping backfill destroys live
data rather than duplicating it. Since INC-2088 there is a guard that rejects such jobs, but
the guard checks the declared range rather than the effective range, and a job with a dynamic
date expression can still get through.

If Delta Store read latency is climbing during a Cinder recovery, that is the retry storm
described in INC-2117. Stagger the restart rather than letting the whole fleet re-read at
once.
