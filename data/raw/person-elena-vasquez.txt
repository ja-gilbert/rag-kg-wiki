Elena Vasquez
Meridian Systems  |  Staff Directory
Last updated: 2025-05-10

Dr. Elena Vasquez is the chief technology officer of Meridian Systems and has been with the
company since its second year.

Elena Vasquez made the original decision to place Ember with the Security Team rather than
the Platform Team, on the argument that the team writing token logic should be the team
accountable for its compromise. That decision is periodically relitigated during incidents
where the Platform Team is paged for an authentication failure it cannot fix.

She chairs the incident review process. All four incidents on record, INC-2041, INC-2088,
INC-2103, and INC-2117, went through reviews she ran, and the standing rule that emerged
from INC-2041, that a dependency on a vendor control plane during service boot is a design
defect, is hers.

She has final sign-off on the Vendor Review Policy and on any contract with a vendor that
sits on a production request path, which currently means Northwind Cloud and Kestrel
Analytics.
