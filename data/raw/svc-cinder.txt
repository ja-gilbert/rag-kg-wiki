Cinder Batch Pipeline
Meridian Systems  |  Service Documentation
Last updated: 2025-09-12

Cinder is the batch processing layer that turns raw event streams into everything downstream
consumes. It runs on a schedule rather than continuously, which is the root of most of its
interesting failure modes.

Cinder is owned by the Data Team. Cinder depends on Delta Store for the feature definitions
and lookup tables its jobs join against. Cinder runs on Northwind Cloud using spot capacity,
which cuts cost by about seventy percent and introduces the eviction behaviour described in
the Cinder runbook.

Jobs are grouped into three tiers. Tier one jobs feed Beacon aggregates and must complete
within one hour. Tier two jobs feed reporting and have a four hour budget. Tier three jobs
are backfills and reprocessing with no hard deadline.

The scheduler has no global backpressure. If tier one jobs fall behind, tier two and three
jobs continue to be submitted, and the queue grows until something manual intervenes. This
exact behaviour produced INC-2117.

Cinder was the cause of INC-2088 and is the service affected by BUG-812.
