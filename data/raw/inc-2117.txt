INC-2117: Cinder Queue Backlog
Meridian Systems  |  Incident Review
Last updated: 2025-10-02

Severity 3. No data loss, but tier one aggregates were stale for eleven hours.

INC-2117 was caused by Cinder. A spot capacity shortage in the primary region evicted
roughly eighty percent of the worker fleet over twenty minutes. Because the Cinder scheduler
has no global backpressure, tier two and tier three jobs continued to be submitted on
schedule while tier one jobs starved behind them in a single shared queue.

INC-2117 affected Delta Store, which absorbed a retry storm as evicted jobs restarted and
re-read their feature definitions. Read latency at the ninety-ninth percentile went from
eleven milliseconds to just over two seconds, which in turn slowed Ember's session lookups
and produced a wave of authentication timeouts that looked, from the outside, like an
entirely separate incident.

INC-2117 was reported by Priya Raman, who noticed the aggregate staleness from the on-call
dashboard rather than from an alert, because the staleness alert had been tuned to a
twelve hour threshold.

Remediation was partial. Priority queues were added, but global backpressure remains
unbuilt and is tracked as ongoing platform debt.
