Atlas API Gateway
Meridian Systems  |  Service Documentation
Last updated: 2025-11-04

Atlas is the public edge for every Meridian Systems product. Every external request enters
through Atlas before it reaches anything else, which makes it the single most load-bearing
component we operate.

Atlas is owned by the Platform Team. Atlas depends on Ember for request authentication, and
a failed Ember call is treated as a hard failure rather than a soft one, so an Ember problem
becomes an Atlas problem within seconds. Atlas runs on Northwind Cloud in three regions,
with us-east-2 carrying roughly sixty percent of production traffic.

The gateway terminates TLS, applies per-tenant rate limits, and emits a structured access
record for every request. Those records are shipped to Beacon for aggregation. Atlas keeps
no durable state of its own; all configuration is pulled at boot from a signed bundle and
refreshed every ninety seconds.

Capacity planning assumes a p99 latency budget of 250 milliseconds end to end, of which
Atlas itself is allowed 40 milliseconds. Sustained breach of that budget is the usual first
signal that something upstream has gone wrong. The Credential Rotation Standard applies to
Atlas because the gateway holds the signing keys used for internal service-to-service calls.

Historical note: Atlas was affected by INC-2041, the longest customer-visible outage in
company history.
