Kestrel Analytics
Meridian Systems  |  Vendor Record
Last updated: 2025-07-05

Kestrel Analytics provides the visualization and dashboarding layer that sits on top of
Beacon. Meridian owns the data; Kestrel owns the interface engineers actually look at.

The Vendor Review Policy governs Kestrel Analytics. The contract runs three years and is
currently in year two, with Elena Vasquez holding sign-off.

The dependency is subtler than the Northwind Cloud one. Kestrel does not sit on a customer
request path, so an outage does not affect the product, but it does sit on the incident
response path. During an incident, engineers cannot see the state of the system without a
working Kestrel. There is no offline fallback beyond raw queries against Beacon, which few
engineers can write under pressure.

Kestrel has read access to Beacon raw events, which is why INC-2103 counted the vendor within
the exposure surface for leaked token material. That finding drove the current requirement
that any vendor with read access to raw telemetry be enumerated in the incident's exposure
analysis by default.
