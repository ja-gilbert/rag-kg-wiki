Vendor Review Policy
Meridian Systems  |  Policy Document
Last updated: 2025-07-01

This policy defines what must be established about a vendor before it may sit on a production
request path, and what must be re-established annually.

The Vendor Review Policy governs Northwind Cloud and governs Kestrel Analytics. It was
authored by Aisha Bello and requires sign-off from Elena Vasquez for any new vendor on a
production path.

The central question the policy forces is not whether the vendor is reliable but what the
vendor can do to us unilaterally, without our consent or notification. Every vendor review
must enumerate the unilateral actions available to the vendor and the blast radius of each.

No service may depend on a vendor control plane during boot. This clause is a direct product
of the finding that a silent instance role revocation could drain a service pool to zero
without any alert firing.

The policy was triggered by INC-2041. Annual re-review is required, and a lapsed review
blocks contract renewal rather than merely flagging it.
