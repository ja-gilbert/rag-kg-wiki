Support: Common Customer Questions
Meridian Systems  |  Internal Reference
Last updated: 2025-11-02

Customers report that the product feels sluggish or unresponsive far more often than they
report that it is down. In almost every case the underlying cause is not where the customer
points.

When a customer says logins are taking a long time, the cause is usually not authentication
capacity. Ember reads session and device metadata from Delta Store on the hot path, so a
Delta Store slowdown appears to the customer as slow sign-in. Check Delta Store read
percentiles first.

When a customer says their numbers look wrong or out of date, distinguish between wrong and
old. Old numbers mean Cinder is behind and aggregates are stale; the system is healthy and
the dashboard is not current. Wrong numbers are far more serious and have historically meant
data loss, as in INC-2088.

When a customer says they are being throttled unpredictably, this is usually the rate limit
counter behaviour described in BUG-771. Counters are per-instance and in memory, and a deploy
seeds them from recent data rather than preserving exact state.

When a customer asks how long we keep their data, the answer comes from Data Retention Policy
v3 and there is no exception path, including for enterprise contracts. Thirty days raw,
thirteen months aggregated, ninety days session metadata.
