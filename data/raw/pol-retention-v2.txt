Data Retention Policy v2
Meridian Systems  |  Policy Document
Last updated: 2024-03-11

Superseded. Retained for historical reference only. Do not apply this policy to current
systems.

Data Retention Policy v2 delegated retention windows to individual teams. Each team declared
its own schedule in a service manifest, subject to a floor of seven days and a ceiling of
twenty-four months.

In practice teams chose windows that suited their own debugging habits, and the declared
manifest values drifted from what the systems actually did. At the time of supersession, three
of five services had manifest values that did not match their real behaviour.

The policy also permitted per-customer extensions negotiated during enterprise deals, which
produced a long tail of bespoke retention commitments that nobody tracked centrally.

This policy was superseded by Data Retention Policy v3 following INC-2088.
