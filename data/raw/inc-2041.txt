INC-2041: Atlas Regional Outage
Meridian Systems  |  Incident Review
Last updated: 2025-06-14

Severity 1. Customer-visible duration was four hours and eleven minutes, the longest in
company history.

INC-2041 was caused by Northwind Cloud. A control plane change in the us-east-2 region
silently revoked the instance role that Atlas uses to fetch its configuration bundle at
boot. Running instances were unaffected, so nothing alerted. When an autoscaling event
replaced instances forty minutes later, the new ones could not boot, and the pool drained
to zero over the following hour.

INC-2041 affected Atlas. Because every external request enters through Atlas, the practical
effect was a total product outage rather than a partial degradation.

INC-2041 was reported by Lin Zhao, who escalated after three enterprise customers opened
tickets within the same ten minute window. Internal alerting did not fire first, which
became the most uncomfortable finding of the review.

INC-2041 was resolved by Marcus Chen, who restored the instance role manually and pinned
the configuration bundle to a local cache so that boot no longer required a live control
plane call.

INC-2041 triggered the Vendor Review Policy. It also produced the standing rule that any
dependency on a vendor control plane during service boot is a design defect.
