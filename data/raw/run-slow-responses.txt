Runbook: Degraded Response Times
Meridian Systems  |  Operations Runbook
Last updated: 2025-10-11

Use this when requests are completing but taking longer than they should, and when the
customer-facing symptom is that the product feels unresponsive rather than that it is down.

First establish where the time is going. Compare the Atlas self-reported gateway time against
total end-to-end time. If the gateway is inside its 40 millisecond allowance, the problem is
downstream and the gateway is merely reporting it.

The most common downstream cause is not the service that appears slow. A Delta Store read
latency increase surfaces as authentication timeouts, because Ember does session lookups on
the hot path, and authentication timeouts surface to the customer as a slow login rather than
as a data problem. Check Delta Store read percentiles before anything else.

Second most common is spot eviction on the Cinder worker fleet producing a retry storm
against Delta Store. The signature is Delta Store latency rising with no corresponding rise
in request volume from the request path.

If aggregates are stale but latency is normal, the product is not slow; the dashboards are
old. That is a different problem and this is the wrong runbook.

Do not restart the gateway. It holds no durable state and restarting it will not help, but it
will reset the rate limit counters and produce a thundering herd.
