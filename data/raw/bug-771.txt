BUG-771: Rate limit counters reset on deploy
Meridian Systems  |  Issue Tracker Export
Last updated: 2025-06-30

Status: fixed. Severity: low. Customer impact: intermittent, brief.

BUG-771 affects Atlas. Rate limit counters are held in memory per instance, so a rolling
deploy resets every counter in the fleet. For a short window after each deploy, a client that
has been throttled is not throttled, and clients that retry aggressively can push a burst
through.

BUG-771 was reported by Lin Zhao after an enterprise customer observed that their own
throttling behaviour changed unpredictably and asked, reasonably, what the actual limit was.

BUG-771 was fixed by Marcus Chen. The fix does not move counters out of memory, which was
judged too expensive at current traffic. Instead the gateway now seeds counters at boot from
a short window of recent Beacon data, which closes most of the gap without introducing a
shared store on the hot path.

The residual behaviour is documented in the Atlas runbook as a known rough edge.
