Nadia Petrova is a security engineer and runs the detection and log review practice. Nadia
Petrova is a member of the Security Team.

Nadia Petrova reported INC-2103, finding full bearer tokens in Beacon during a routine log
sampling review nine days after the offending statement shipped. She also reported BUG-903.

The log sampling practice she runs is manual and scheduled weekly, which is the reason the
exposure ran for nine days rather than one. She has argued for automated secret scanning on
the ingestion path into Beacon rather than sampling after the fact, and that work is funded
but not yet started.

She maintains the internal catalogue of what each service is permitted to log, which is the
document teams are supposed to consult before adding instrumentation and which almost nobody
consults before adding instrumentation.
