On-Call Policy
Meridian Systems  |  Policy Document
Last updated: 2025-04-19

This policy defines rotation structure, compensation, and the limits of what may be asked of
an on-call engineer.

The On-Call Policy governs the Platform Team and governs the Data Team. It was authored by
Priya Raman.

Rotations are one week. There is a primary who is expected to respond within five minutes and
a secondary who is expected to be reachable within thirty and who is explicitly not expected
to be awake. No engineer may be primary more than one week in four.

Any page between 22:00 and 07:00 local time entitles the engineer to a compensating day off,
taken at their discretion and not requiring approval. Any rotation with more than three night
pages triggers a mandatory review of the alerting that caused them.

Teams may run a modified rotation with the lead's sign-off. The Data Team runs one combined
rotation across three services under this clause, which the policy permits and which the
policy's author has said on record she considers a bad idea.
