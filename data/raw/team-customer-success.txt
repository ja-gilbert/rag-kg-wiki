Last updated: 2025-05-02

Customer Success is the first human contact point when something is wrong, and in practice
it has been the first detection point for two of the four incidents on record.

Customer Success is led by Lin Zhao. Rafael Ortiz is a member of Customer Success and works
as the solutions architect for enterprise accounts.

The team does not own any service. It does own the escalation path: any ticket that mentions
errors, latency, or missing data is escalated to the owning team's on-call within fifteen
minutes rather than being triaged first.

That rule exists because of INC-2041, which Lin Zhao reported after three enterprise
customers opened tickets before any internal alert fired. The review found that the previous
triage-first process had added twenty-five minutes to detection.

Rafael Ortiz reported INC-2088, again from a customer signal rather than an internal one.
The pattern is well known internally and is the strongest argument in the current push to
fund better data-quality alerting.
