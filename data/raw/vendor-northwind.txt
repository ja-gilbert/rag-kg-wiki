Northwind Cloud
Meridian Systems  |  Vendor Record
Last updated: 2025-07-05

Northwind Cloud is the infrastructure provider for all Meridian production workloads. Atlas,
Ember, Beacon, Cinder, and Delta Store all run on Northwind Cloud.

The Vendor Review Policy governs Northwind Cloud. Elena Vasquez holds sign-off on the
contract, which is renewed annually and which is currently in its third year.

Northwind Cloud caused INC-2041 through a control plane change that silently revoked an
instance role. The vendor's own status page showed no incident throughout, because from their
perspective nothing failed; a role was changed, as designed, by a control plane operation.
That distinction is the reason the Vendor Review Policy now asks what a vendor can do to us
unilaterally rather than asking how reliable the vendor is.

Spot capacity from Northwind Cloud underlies the Cinder worker fleet and produced the
eviction wave in INC-2117. Spot pricing saves roughly seventy percent on batch compute and
the eviction behaviour is considered a fair trade, given backpressure work is possible.

Region coverage is three regions, with us-east-2 carrying about sixty percent of production
traffic.
