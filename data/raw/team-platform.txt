Platform Team
Meridian Systems  |  Team Handbook
Last updated: 2025-05-02

The Platform Team owns the runtime substrate every other team builds on: the gateway, the
deployment tooling, and the shared libraries for configuration and observability.

The Platform Team is led by Priya Raman. Marcus Chen is a member of the Platform Team and
acts as the technical lead for gateway work specifically.

The team owns Atlas. It does not own Ember, despite Atlas depending on it, and this split
has been a recurring source of friction during incidents because the team that gets paged
for a login failure is not the team that can fix it.

The On-Call Policy governs the Platform Team. The team runs a one-week primary rotation with
a secondary who is expected to be reachable but not awake.

Current headcount is nine engineers. Two open roles have been unfilled for two quarters,
which is the stated reason global backpressure work on the Cinder scheduler has not been
picked up even though it sits on the platform debt list.
