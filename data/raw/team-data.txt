Data Team
Meridian Systems  |  Team Handbook
Last updated: 2025-05-02

The Data Team owns everything between raw event ingestion and the numbers a customer sees
in a report.

The Data Team is led by Jonas Weber. The team owns Beacon, Cinder, and Delta Store, which
makes it the only team at Meridian responsible for three production services at once.

The On-Call Policy governs the Data Team, but the team runs a modified rotation: a single
on-call engineer covers all three services, on the theory that the failure modes are so
interlinked that splitting the rotation would create handoff gaps. INC-2117 is usually cited
as evidence that this theory is correct, since diagnosing it required holding Cinder, Delta
Store, and Beacon in mind simultaneously.

The Data Retention Policy v3 was drafted primarily by this team following INC-2088.

Headcount is seven engineers plus one analyst. The analyst role reports into the team but is
functionally shared with Customer Success.
