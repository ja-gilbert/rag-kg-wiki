Delta Store Feature Store
Meridian Systems  |  Service Documentation
Last updated: 2025-08-21

Delta Store holds the canonical feature and reference data that other services read at
request time. It is the closest thing Meridian has to a shared database, and it is treated
with corresponding caution.

Delta Store is owned by the Data Team. Delta Store runs on Northwind Cloud with synchronous
replication across two regions and asynchronous replication to a third.

Two services read from Delta Store on the hot path: Ember, for session and device metadata,
and Cinder, for feature definitions. That combination means a Delta Store degradation shows
up simultaneously as an authentication problem and a data pipeline problem, which has caused
misdiagnosis more than once.

Writes are append-only with a compaction pass that runs nightly. Readers always see a
consistent snapshot; the tradeoff is that a write is not visible to readers for up to ten
seconds.

The Data Retention Policy v3 governs Delta Store. Delta Store was affected by INC-2117.
