Credential Rotation Standard
Meridian Systems  |  Policy Document
Last updated: 2025-08-30

This standard defines rotation intervals, revocation expectations, and logging prohibitions
for anything that functions as a credential.

The Credential Rotation Standard governs Ember and governs Atlas. It was authored by Tomas
Oliveira and was triggered by INC-2103.

Signing keys rotate every ninety days with a fourteen day overlap window during which both
the old and new key validate. Machine credentials rotate every thirty days. Interactive
session tokens are not rotated; they expire in fifteen minutes and are reissued.

No credential material may appear in any log at any level. This is stated without exception
because the incident that produced this standard involved a debug statement that a reasonable
engineer added for a reasonable reason. The enforcement mechanism is a pre-commit check on
the auth path rather than a review expectation.

Revocation is eventually consistent with a worst case of thirty seconds. The standard
acknowledges this explicitly so that reviewers stop rediscovering it as though it were a
defect.
