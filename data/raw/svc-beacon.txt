Beacon Telemetry Platform
Meridian Systems  |  Service Documentation
Last updated: 2025-09-30

Beacon is where operational truth lives. Every service emits metrics, traces, and structured
events into Beacon, and every dashboard and alert we run reads from it.

Beacon is owned by the Data Team. Beacon depends on Cinder to roll raw event streams into
the hourly and daily aggregates that dashboards actually query. When Cinder is unhealthy,
Beacon continues to accept writes but its aggregates go stale, which is a failure mode that
has repeatedly fooled on-call engineers into believing the system is fine.

Beacon runs on Northwind Cloud. The visualization layer is provided by Kestrel Analytics
under a three-year contract, which means a portion of our observability stack is a vendor
dependency rather than something we control.

Raw events are retained for thirty days and aggregates for thirteen months. Those numbers
are set by the Data Retention Policy v3 and are not adjustable per team, a change from the
previous regime where each team picked its own window.

Beacon was affected by INC-2088, in which roughly four hours of raw events were lost.
