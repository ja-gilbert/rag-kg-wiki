Internal Glossary
Meridian Systems  |  Internal Reference
Last updated: 2025-11-20

Aggregate staleness. The gap between the newest event in Beacon and the newest event
reflected in a computed aggregate. Normal is under one hour for tier one. The alert threshold
is twelve hours, which INC-2117 showed to be far too loose.

Blast radius. The set of things a failure can reach. Used in vendor reviews to describe what a
unilateral vendor action can affect.

Cross-team exception. The process by which an engineer may change a service owned by another
team, requiring a reviewer from the owning team. BUG-903 was fixed under this exception.

Hard failure. A dependency failure treated as fatal rather than degraded. Atlas treats a
failed Ember call as a hard failure.

Hot path. The synchronous request path a customer waits on. Atlas, Ember, and Delta Store are
on it. Beacon and Cinder are not.

Latency budget. The 250 millisecond p99 end-to-end allowance, of which Atlas may consume 40.
Aisha Bello approves any change that spends more of it.

Tier one, two, three. Cinder job classes with one hour, four hour, and no deadline
respectively.

Unilateral action. Something a vendor can do without our consent or notification. The
central question of the Vendor Review Policy.
