INC-2088: Beacon Event Loss
Meridian Systems  |  Incident Review
Last updated: 2025-07-29

Severity 2. Roughly four hours of raw telemetry events were permanently lost.

INC-2088 was caused by Cinder. A tier three backfill job was submitted with a date range
that overlapped the live ingestion window. Because the pipeline treats its output partitions
as idempotent overwrites rather than merges, the backfill rewrote partitions that live
ingestion was still filling, and the overwritten events were unrecoverable.

INC-2088 affected Beacon. Dashboards showed a clean but wrong picture: the aggregates
recomputed successfully over the truncated data, so nothing looked broken. The gap was
noticed only because a customer's usage report did not match their own logs.

INC-2088 was reported by Rafael Ortiz after a customer escalation. INC-2088 was resolved by
Jonas Weber, who halted the backfill fleet and added a guard rejecting any job whose output
range intersects the live window.

INC-2088 triggered the Data Retention Policy v3. The review concluded that per-team
retention windows made it impossible to reason about what had actually been lost, which is
why v3 centralized retention.
