Marcus Chen is a staff engineer and the technical lead for gateway work. Marcus Chen is a
member of the Platform Team.

Marcus Chen resolved INC-2041, restoring the revoked instance role and eliminating the boot
time dependency on the vendor control plane. That fix, pinning the configuration bundle to a
local cache, is now the standard pattern for any service that must survive a vendor control
plane failure.

Marcus Chen fixed BUG-771 and also fixed BUG-903, which is notable because BUG-903 sits in
Ember, a service owned by the Security Team rather than his own. The fix was made under the
cross-team exception in the review process, with Tomas Oliveira as reviewer.

He is the author of the internal shared configuration library that Atlas, Beacon, and Ember
all consume, which makes him an informal dependency for a great deal of work that is not
formally assigned to him.
