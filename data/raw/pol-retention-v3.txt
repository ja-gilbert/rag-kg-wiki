Data Retention Policy v3
Meridian Systems  |  Policy Document
Last updated: 2025-09-08

This policy sets a single company-wide retention schedule. It replaces the previous regime in
which each team selected its own windows.

Data Retention Policy v3 supersedes Data Retention Policy v2. The policy governs Beacon and
governs Delta Store, and applies to any future service that stores customer-derived data.

Raw telemetry events are retained for thirty days. Aggregates derived from those events are
retained for thirteen months. Session and device metadata is retained for ninety days after
last activity. There is no per-team and no per-customer exception path, which is deliberate
and which is the most frequently contested clause in the document.

The rationale is auditability rather than cost. The review of INC-2088 could not determine
what had been lost because no one could establish what had existed, and the drafters
concluded that a uniform schedule was a precondition for ever answering that question again.

The policy was triggered by INC-2088 and was drafted by Jonas Weber. Storage spend fell about
eighteen percent as a side effect.
