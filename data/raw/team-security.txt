Security Team
Meridian Systems  |  Team Handbook
Last updated: 2025-05-02

The Security Team owns authentication, authorization, secrets handling, and the review
process that gates any change touching those areas.

The Security Team is led by Tomas Oliveira. Nadia Petrova is a member of the Security Team
and runs the detection and log review practice.

The team owns Ember. This is unusual: at most companies the auth service sits with platform
engineering. At Meridian it was deliberately placed with security after an early decision
that the team writing the token logic should be the team accountable for its compromise.

The Credential Rotation Standard was authored by Tomas Oliveira on behalf of this team.

The team is small, four engineers, and its stated operating model is that it should be a
reviewer and toolmaker rather than a bottleneck. In practice the review queue for auth-path
changes runs about three days, which teams route around by batching changes, which is the
opposite of what the policy intends.
