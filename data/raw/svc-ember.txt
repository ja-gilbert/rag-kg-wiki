Ember Authentication Service
Meridian Systems  |  Service Documentation
Last updated: 2025-10-19

Ember issues and validates the tokens that every other Meridian service trusts. It is a
small service by line count and an enormous one by blast radius.

Ember is owned by the Security Team. Ember depends on Delta Store for session and device
metadata lookups. Ember runs on Northwind Cloud, deliberately in the same regions as Atlas
so that a regional failure takes out both together rather than leaving Atlas alive but
unable to authenticate anyone.

Tokens are short-lived by design: fifteen minutes for interactive sessions, sixty minutes
for machine credentials. Refresh is handled by a separate endpoint that is rate limited far
more aggressively than the issue path. Ember never logs raw token material, a rule added
after INC-2103.

The service exposes three endpoints that matter operationally: /issue, /introspect, and
/revoke. Revocation is eventually consistent with a worst case of thirty seconds, which is
a deliberate tradeoff and a recurring source of confusion during security reviews.

The Credential Rotation Standard governs Ember. Ember was the origin of INC-2103 and is
also the service affected by BUG-903.
